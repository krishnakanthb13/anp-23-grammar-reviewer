### LinkedIn
Excited to announce the initial release of the Grammar & Style Reviewer Plugin for Amplenote! 🧑‍🏫✨

Writing inside your knowledge base should be fast, thoughtful, and private. Most AI writing assistants either aggressively overwrite entire notes or lock you into one expensive proprietary model.

The Grammar & Style Reviewer brings complete, user-in-the-loop editing directly into Amplenote:
• Multi-AI Agility: Support for 8 providers including OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek Direct, Ollama (Local/Cloud), OpenAI, and Anthropic Claude.
• Interactive Visual Diffing: Side-by-side comparison of additions and deletions with full control to Accept, Reject, Edit, or Re-Review every sentence or paragraph.
• 6 Dynamic Aesthetic Themes: Instantly switch between Midnight, Nord, Glassmorphism, Emerald, Cyber Violet, and Clean Daylight with matching themed scrollbars.
• Zero-Latency Architecture: Instant client-side tab switching and local-first session resilience that survives browser refreshes.
• Audit Trail: Generates clear revision notes and machine-readable logs so you never lose past iterations.

Check out the open-source repository and installation guide on GitHub:

### Reddit
**Suggested Subreddits:** `r/Amplenote`, `r/PKM`, `r/NoteTaking`, `r/Productivity`, `r/SideProject`, `r/webdev`

**Title:** I built an open-source Multi-AI Grammar & Style Reviewer plugin for Amplenote (with visual diffs, local Ollama support & 6 dynamic themes)

**Post:**
Hey everyone!

One thing I've always wanted in Amplenote is an AI copyeditor that doesn't just blindly overwrite my paragraphs, but actually gives me a granular side-by-side diff where I can accept, reject, edit, or re-prompt each suggestion chunk by chunk.

I just released the open-source **Grammar & Style Reviewer Plugin** (`anp-23-grammar-reviewer`).

### Key Highlights:
1. **Multi-AI Provider Flexibility**: Out-of-the-box support for 8 AI backends:
   - Free tier / affordable options: OpenRouter free models (GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek V4 Flash), Google Gemini (Flash-Lite/3.7 Flash), Groq (300+ tok/s), and Mistral.
   - 100% Private / Offline: Ollama (Localhost & Cloud models).
   - Frontier models: Anthropic Claude (Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5), DeepSeek V4 Pro, and OpenAI GPT-5.6.
2. **Interactive Visual Diffs**: Clear `<ins>` / `<del>` highlighting with power hotkeys (`A` Accept, `R` Reject, `N`/`P` Next/Prev, `T` Theme).
3. **6 Switchable Themes**: Includes Midnight Slate, Nord Arctic, Glassmorphism, Emerald Forest, Cyber Violet, and Clean Daylight with matching custom themed scrollbars.
4. **Zero-Latency Client Tabs**: Fast 0ms tab switching between Reviewer, History Logs, and Settings.
5. **Full Audit Logging**: Automatically preserves committed changes under `-reports/-grammar/-changes` with source backlinks, plus machine-readable history logs under `-reports/-grammar/-history`.

All code is open-source under GPL v3.
